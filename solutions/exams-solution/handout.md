# MRI II — Oral Exam: Structure & Grading
 
## Format
 
At the start of your preparation time, you draw **one card: the
Category I card**. This card tells you which Jupyter notebook to
complete during the 30-minute preparation window. Bring your
completed notebook — and the image it produces — with you to the
oral exam.
 
The **Category II and Category III cards** are drawn later, at the
oral exam table itself, and are answered on the spot.
 
Each card contains **3 questions** that build on each other, moving
from a broader question toward more specific ones on the same topic.
No card requires knowledge from any other card — you can answer each
one fully on its own.
 
## Points
 
| Category | Points | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I (notebook + discussion) | 20 (14 + 3 + 3) | The first question also covers discussing and evaluating the practical task you completed during preparation |
| II | 10 (4 + 3 + 3) | — |
| III | 10 (4 + 3 + 3) | — |
| **Total** | **40** | |
 
## Joker
 
You may **redraw one card, once** — but only for the **Category II
or Category III** card drawn at the exam table. The Category I card
cannot be redrawn: a redraw would mean switching to a different
notebook with no time left to complete it.
 
## Grading
 
Every question is graded with **partial credit** — you do not need a
perfect, complete answer to earn points. Answering spontaneously and
thoroughly earns full credit; the examiner may ask a brief follow-up
question to clarify or probe your understanding further on a
specific point.
